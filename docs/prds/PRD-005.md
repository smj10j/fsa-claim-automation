# PRD-005: FSA Budget Dashboard & Year-End Alerts

**Status**: `draft`
**Priority**: P1
**Effort**: M (3–5 days)
**Dependencies**: PRD-003
**Last updated**: 2026-03-18

---

## Problem

The #1 reason people lose FSA money is not knowing how much they have left or when they need to spend it. Most FSA portals have poor UX around balance visibility, and users don't know about grace periods or run-out-and-use-it rules until it's too late. An extension that proactively surfaces this information creates massive, tangible value — this is the feature most likely to drive word-of-mouth and willingness to pay.

---

## Goals

1. Display the user's current FSA balance prominently in the popup.
2. Show a spending timeline: how much was elected, claimed, pending, and remaining.
3. Alert users proactively as year-end or grace period deadlines approach.
4. Help users estimate how much they need to spend before the deadline.

## Non-Goals

- Financial advice or investment guidance
- Integration with employer HR systems
- Support for HSA or DCFSA (only FSA for now)

## Functional Requirements

### FR-1: Balance Scraping from Navia
- Content script injected on `navia.com` (after user visits portal) reads:
  - `availableBalance: number` — available to spend today
  - `electedAmount: number` — total annual election
  - `claimedYTD: number` — year-to-date approved reimbursements
  - `pendingAmount: number` — submitted but not yet approved
- Store as `FsaBalance` object in `chrome.storage.local` with a `lastRefreshedAt` timestamp.
- Scraping is passive (triggered when user is on navia.com, not proactive navigation).

### FR-2: Balance Display in Popup
- Popup home screen (before scan starts) shows a "Your FSA" card with:
  - Available balance (large, prominent number)
  - Segmented progress bar: Used | Pending | Available
  - "Refreshed X minutes ago" with a manual refresh button
  - Year-end date with days remaining (e.g., "72 days left")

### FR-3: Year-End Countdown & Alerts
- Extension alarm (`chrome.alarms`) triggers daily at 9 AM.
- At 60, 30, 14, 7, 3, and 1 days before year-end (and grace period end), fire a `chrome.notifications` alert:
  - "You have $342 left in your FSA. Your deadline is in 14 days."
  - Notification click opens the popup.
- Snooze option: user can dismiss for 3 or 7 days.

### FR-4: Spend Estimate
- Compute `recommendedSpend = availableBalance - pendingAmount`.
- If `recommendedSpend > 0` and deadline < 30 days, show a callout:
  - "You have ~$342 to spend before [date]. Start a scan to find eligible items."
  - CTA button: "Scan Amazon Orders" — triggers PRD-001 flow.

### FR-5: Grace Period Support
- User can configure grace period in settings (see PRD-010):
  - None (strict year-end)
  - 2.5-month grace period (common plan type)
  - Rollover amount (some plans roll over up to $660)
- Dashboard shows correct deadline based on configured plan type.
- "Grace Period Active" badge shown during grace period window.

### FR-6: Extension-Claimed Breakdown
- Show how much has been claimed through this extension vs. total claimed.
- Derived from `AppState.claims` where `status: 'submitted'`.
- Example: "You've claimed $487 through this extension (of $1,200 total claimed)."

### FR-7: Manual Balance Override
- If balance scraping fails or user doesn't use Navia, allow manual entry of:
  - Available balance
  - Year-end date
- Manual entries flagged with "Manually entered" indicator and date.

## Technical Notes

- `chrome.alarms` persists across service worker restarts — ideal for deadline alerts.
- `chrome.notifications` requires the `notifications` permission in manifest.
- Balance scraping must be passive (triggered by `content_scripts` matching `navia.com/*`) not active navigation.
- Year-end date should be stored in user settings and default to Dec 31 for calendar-year plans.
- The "days remaining" calculation must account for timezone — use local midnight, not UTC.
