# Navia Benefits — Health Care FSA Eligible Expenses

Source: Navia Benefit Solutions eligible expenses list (Health Care FSA filter: covered + LMN + prescription + not covered).
729 total entries as of 2026-03-18.

**Status key:**
- `covered` — Eligible, no extra documentation needed
- `lmn` — Eligible with a Letter of Medical Necessity from a provider
- `prescription` — Eligible with a valid prescription
- `not-covered` — Explicitly ineligible
